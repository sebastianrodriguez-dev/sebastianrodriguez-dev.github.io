Supported features

Accessibility

The four-card order, clues, feedback, hints, records, settings, and purchase controls use text and labeled controls rather than decorative art alone.

Larger Text

All Four in Place uses Dynamic Type. At accessibility text sizes, the four-card order changes from a row to a vertical list so item names have room to grow.

Move the four cards

Each card exposes its item name and numbered position to iOS accessibility services. You can drag a card, tap a card and then its destination, or use named actions to move left, right, or to position 1, 2, 3, or 4.

VoiceOver and Voice Control

Controls provide labels, values, hints, headings, and stable actions for VoiceOver and Voice Control. Item names remain visible beside the semantic art.

Appearance and color

The app supports Light and Dark appearance. Text labels, shapes, order numbers, and status wording carry meaning that is not left to color alone. Visual collections change palette and framing while keeping each item's identity.

Motion, sound, and haptics

Turn on Reduce motion in Settings. The app also reads the system Reduce Motion preference and limits card and result movement when either setting calls for it.

Sound and haptics can each be turned off. No clue or answer depends on sound or haptics.

Hints and feedback

Show a step presents a written reasoning step. Helpful, Classic, and Strict assistance levels change the amount of written feedback after an incorrect check. Changing the level does not reset the current puzzle.

Get help

If a label, action, text size, appearance, or motion setting does not work for you, email all-four-in-place-support.78efy@passmail.net. Include the screen, app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the accessibility setting you were using.

See Support for gameplay and purchase help, and read the privacy policy before sending a message.
