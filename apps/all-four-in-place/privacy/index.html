Privacy policy

Effective August 9, 2026

This policy explains how All Four in Place handles information when you use the iPhone app or ask for support.

Short version

The app has no player account, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or developer backend. Puzzle play and settings stay on your device. Apple processes App Store purchases. If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to send.

Information stored on your device

All Four in Place stores these kinds of information locally:

  • campaign and daily puzzle progress;
  • current puzzle sessions and card orders;
  • first-check and no-hint records;
  • daily streaks and completion dates;
  • assistance, sound, haptics, Reduce Motion, and visual-collection settings;
  • earned chapter seals and visual collections.

The app stores this information in its Application Support area. It is not sent to a developer server and does not sync through a developer account.

After a verified Full Game purchase, the app also keeps a device-only Keychain entitlement record. That record can contain the product identifier, Apple transaction identifiers, purchase and verification dates, and signed verification data needed to preserve and check access. The developer does not receive that Keychain record.

App Store purchases

Full Game is a non-consumable in-app purchase handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles the Apple Account, payment method, billing details, purchase history, refund, and related App Store data under Apple's privacy policy.

The app reads Apple's verified transaction state on the device to grant or remove Full Game access. It has no developer purchase server and does not send receipts or transaction records to the developer.

Sharing

The result Share sheet opens only when you choose it. The shared text does not include the puzzle answer. You choose the destination and recipients through iOS. The app does not receive the chosen destination or a copy of what the destination does with the message.

The destination app or service applies its own privacy terms.

Support messages

If you contact support, the channel you choose may send the developer information such as your email address, message, screenshots, device and iOS details, app version, or purchase context. Send only what is needed to explain the problem.

Do not send a password, Apple Account verification code, full payment-card number, or complete purchase receipt.

The developer uses support information to answer the request, diagnose the reported problem, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations. It is kept only as long as needed for those purposes, then deleted or de-identified unless law requires longer retention. The email, hosting, or other provider used for support may process the message under its own terms.

Support website

These public support pages use no JavaScript, forms, cookies, analytics, or remote assets. The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process request and security logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Data the developer does not collect from the app

The current app does not send the developer:

  • contact information;
  • precise or coarse location;
  • contacts, photos, audio, camera, health, or fitness data;
  • browsing or search history;
  • advertising or device identifiers;
  • product-interaction analytics;
  • diagnostics or crash reports through a third-party SDK;
  • purchase or payment information through a developer server;
  • content from your puzzles, records, or settings.

All Four in Place does not track you across apps or websites and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Deleting local information

Deleting the app removes information in the app's local container. iOS Keychain items can remain after an app is removed, so the local Full Game entitlement record may remain on the device. Apple also keeps App Store purchase history under its own policy so an eligible purchase can be restored.

Deleting local app data does not delete a support message already sent or Apple's purchase records. Use the support address below for a request about support correspondence. Use Apple's account and purchase tools for information held by Apple.

Children

The app does not offer accounts, chat, targeted advertising, or developer-side profiling. It does not knowingly collect a child's personal information from the app. The App Store age rating describes content suitability; it does not change the data practices in this policy.

Security

Local app data is protected by iOS and the device's security settings. The Full Game entitlement record uses the iOS Keychain and is marked for this device. No method of storage is risk-free, so keep iOS current and use a device passcode.

Changes to this policy

The policy may change when the app's features, support provider, or legal duties change. The effective date at the top will be updated. A material change must be reflected in the app's App Privacy answers and release records before the changed build is submitted.
