Privacy policy

Privacy

The app has no player account, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or developer backend. Puzzle play and settings stay on your device.

Effective August 9, 2026.

Information stored on your device

All Four in Place stores campaign and daily progress, current card orders, records, streaks, settings, chapter seals, and visual collections on the device. The app does not send this information to a developer server or sync it through a developer account.

After a verified Full Game purchase, the app keeps a device-only Keychain record to preserve and check access. It can include the product and Apple transaction identifiers, purchase and verification dates, and signed verification data. The developer does not receive this record.

App Store purchases

Apple processes the Full Game purchase through StoreKit. Apple handles the Apple Account, payment method, billing details, purchase history, refund, and related App Store data under Apple's privacy policy.

The app reads Apple's verified transaction state on the device to grant or remove Full Game access. It has no purchase server and does not send receipts or transaction records to the developer.

Sharing

The result Share sheet opens only when you choose it. Shared text does not include the puzzle answer. You choose the destination and recipients through iOS. The destination app or service applies its own privacy terms.

Support messages

If you contact support, the developer receives what you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshot, device and iOS details, app version, or purchase context. Send only what is needed to explain the problem.

Support information is used to answer the request, diagnose the reported problem, prevent misuse, and meet legal duties. It is kept only as long as needed for those purposes, then deleted or de-identified unless law requires longer retention. Email or hosting providers may process the message under their own terms.

Do not send a password, Apple Account verification code, full payment-card number, or complete purchase receipt.

Support website

These public pages use no JavaScript, forms, cookies, analytics, or remote visual assets. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process request and security logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Information the app does not send to the developer

  • contact information from the app;
  • location, contacts, photos, audio, camera, health, or fitness data;
  • browsing or search history;
  • advertising or device identifiers;
  • product-interaction analytics or third-party crash reports;
  • purchase or payment information through a developer server;
  • puzzle, record, or settings content.

The app does not track you across apps or websites and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Deleting local information

Deleting the app removes information in its local container. iOS Keychain items can remain after an app is removed, so the device-only Full Game record may remain. Apple also keeps App Store purchase history under its own policy so an eligible purchase can be restored.

Deleting local app data does not delete a support message already sent or records held by Apple. Contact support about correspondence sent to the developer. Use Apple's account and purchase tools for information held by Apple.

Children

The app offers no accounts, chat, targeted advertising, or developer-side profiling. It does not knowingly collect a child's personal information from the app. The App Store age rating describes content suitability; it does not change these data practices.

Security

iOS and the device's security settings protect local app data. The device-only Full Game record uses iOS Keychain. No storage method is risk-free, so keep iOS current and use a device passcode.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the app's features, support provider, or legal duties change. The effective date will be updated when that happens.
