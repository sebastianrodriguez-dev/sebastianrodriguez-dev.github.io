Support

Read the clues, move four cards into order, and check your answer when every clue is true.

Contact support

Email all-four-in-place-support.78efy@passmail.net.

Start a puzzle

  • Open Today for the current daily puzzle and available daily history.
  • Open Chapters for the campaign. Chapter 1 contains 24 free puzzles that teach the method.
  • Open Settings > How to play for the three basic steps.

Every puzzle has one correct order. You can ask for a reasoning step without spending a token or watching an ad.

Move the cards

  • Drag a card to another position, or tap one card and then tap the position where you want it to move.
  • With VoiceOver, focus a card and use its actions to move left, move right, or move to a numbered position.
  • At accessibility text sizes, the four positions change to a vertical layout so item names can grow.

Tap Check order when you think every clue is true. An incorrect check follows the selected assistance level:

  • Helpful points out more about what needs attention.
  • Classic gives a moderate response.
  • Strict gives the least information.

Change the level from the puzzle options or Settings > Assistance level. Changing it does not reset the current puzzle.

Hints and reasoning

Tap Show a step for one useful next step. Hints are unlimited. After a solve, open Why it works to review the short reasoning path that proves the final order.

Using a hint affects the no-hint record for that attempt, but it does not block the puzzle or require a purchase.

Daily puzzles

The fixed daily schedule contains 1,095 solver-verified puzzles dated from August 1, 2026, through July 30, 2029.

Without Full Game, you can play today's puzzle and the previous seven available daily puzzles. Full Game opens every daily puzzle available by date. Future puzzles remain unavailable until their date.

If the shown date looks wrong, check the iPhone's date, time, and time-zone settings, then close and reopen the app.

Full Game

Full Game is one non-consumable in-app purchase. It opens:

  • Chapters 2–5, bringing the campaign to 120 puzzles;
  • every daily puzzle available in the archive;
  • visual collections earned by completing Chapters 2–5.

The purchase screen shows Apple's current localized price. All free puzzles, hints, assistance levels, records, and free-play collections remain usable without the purchase.

Buy Full Game

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Full Game.
  3. Review what is included and the price shown by Apple.
  4. Use the purchase button and follow the App Store confirmation.

A pending purchase unlocks after Apple approves and verifies it. Canceling the App Store sheet does not charge or unlock the game.

Restore Full Game

  1. Connect the iPhone to the internet.
  2. Make sure the App Store is using the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  3. Open Settings > Full Game.
  4. Tap Restore Purchases and follow any Apple prompt.

If no purchase is found, confirm the Apple Account and purchase history. Family Sharing is enabled for the live Full Game product. Access still depends on Apple's purchase eligibility, family group, account, storefront, and Family Sharing rules.

If Apple refunded or revoked the transaction, Full Game access can be removed. Use Apple's purchase-support site for a charge or refund question.

Purchase troubleshooting

The price or purchase button does not load

  • Check the internet connection.
  • Confirm that in-app purchases are allowed by Screen Time or device management.
  • Confirm that the App Store is signed in and available in the current region.
  • Close and reopen the Full Game screen.
  • Check Apple's System Status if StoreKit remains unavailable.

The purchase is pending

Ask to Buy, payment review, or another Apple approval can leave a purchase pending. Keep the app installed. Full Game unlocks when Apple provides a verified transaction.

Restore says no purchase was found

  • Check that the same Apple Account made the purchase.
  • Check the App Store purchase history.
  • Try again on a stable connection.
  • If Apple shows the purchase but the app still cannot restore it, report the app version, iOS version, exact on-screen message, and approximate purchase date.

Progress and local data

Puzzle progress, settings, records, streaks, and sessions are stored on the device. The app has no player account or cloud-sync service. Deleting the app can remove local progress. Apple's purchase history can restore an eligible Full Game purchase; it does not restore campaign records or daily streaks.

Accessibility and appearance

The app supports large Dynamic Type layouts, Light and Dark appearance, a Reduce Motion setting, text labels beside semantic item art, and named card-move actions exposed to iOS accessibility services.

To limit movement, open Settings and turn on Reduce motion. The app also respects the system Reduce Motion setting during puzzle and result movement.

Read the accessibility statement for verified review scope and limits.

Report a problem

Include:

  • what you were trying to do;
  • the exact message shown;
  • the app version and build shown by iOS;
  • iPhone model and iOS version;
  • whether the issue occurs in Today, Chapters, a puzzle, Settings, or Full Game;
  • a screenshot if it does not reveal private account or payment information.

Never send a password, Apple Account verification code, full payment-card number, or complete purchase receipt.
