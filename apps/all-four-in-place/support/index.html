Player help

Support

Read the clues, move four cards into order, and check your answer when every clue is true.

Need a hand? Email all-four-in-place-support.78efy@passmail.net.

Start a puzzle

  • Open Today for the current daily puzzle and available recent puzzles.
  • Open Chapters for the campaign. Chapter 1 has 24 free puzzles that teach the method.
  • Open Settings → How to play for the three basic steps.

Every puzzle has one correct order. Hints do not use tokens or require an ad.

Move the cards

Drag a card to another position, or tap a card and then tap the position where you want it to move. With VoiceOver, focus a card and use its actions to move left, right, or to a numbered position.

Tap Check order when you think every clue is true. If the order is wrong, the response follows your assistance level:

  • Helpful points out more about what needs attention.
  • Classic gives a moderate response.
  • Strict gives the least information.

Change the level from the puzzle options or Settings → Assistance level. It will not reset the puzzle.

Hints and reasoning

Tap Show a step for one useful next move. After a solve, open Why it works to walk through the reasoning. Using a hint changes the no-hint record for that attempt, but it does not block the puzzle.

Daily puzzles

A new puzzle becomes available each day. Without Full Game, you can play today's puzzle and the previous seven available daily puzzles. Full Game opens every daily puzzle available by date. Future puzzles stay locked until their date.

If the date looks wrong, check the iPhone's date, time, and time-zone settings, then close and reopen the app.

Full Game

Full Game is one non-consumable in-app purchase. It opens Chapters 2–5, every daily puzzle available by date, and the visual collections earned through those chapters. The purchase screen shows Apple's current localized price.

Buy Full Game

  1. Open Settings → Full Game.
  2. Review what is included and the price shown by Apple.
  3. Use the purchase button and follow the App Store confirmation.

A pending purchase unlocks after Apple approves and verifies it. Canceling the App Store sheet does not charge or unlock the game.

Restore Full Game

  1. Connect the iPhone to the internet.
  2. Make sure the App Store uses the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  3. Open Settings → Full Game.
  4. Tap Restore Purchases and follow any Apple prompt.

If no purchase is found, confirm the Apple Account and purchase history. Family Sharing access still depends on Apple's purchase eligibility, family group, account, storefront, and sharing rules.

Apple manages purchase history, refunds, billing, and payment methods. Use Apple's purchase-support site for a charge or refund question.

Purchase troubleshooting

The price or purchase button does not load

  • Check the internet connection.
  • Confirm that Screen Time or device management allows in-app purchases.
  • Confirm that the App Store is signed in and available in your region.
  • Close and reopen the Full Game screen.
  • Check Apple's System Status if the store remains unavailable.

The purchase is pending

Ask to Buy, payment review, or another Apple approval can leave a purchase pending. Keep the app installed. Full Game unlocks when Apple provides a verified transaction.

Restore says no purchase was found

  • Check that the same Apple Account made the purchase.
  • Check the App Store purchase history.
  • Try again on a stable connection.
  • If Apple shows the purchase but the app still cannot restore it, send support the app version, iOS version, exact message, and approximate purchase date.

Progress and local data

Puzzle progress, settings, records, streaks, and current sessions stay on the device. The app has no player account or cloud sync. Deleting the app can remove local progress. Apple's purchase history can restore an eligible Full Game purchase, but it does not restore campaign records or daily streaks.

Accessibility and appearance

All Four in Place supports Larger Text, Light and Dark appearance, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, Voice Control, visible text labels, and named card-move actions. Read the accessibility statement for details.

Report a problem

Email all-four-in-place-support.78efy@passmail.net and include:

  • what you were trying to do and the exact message shown;
  • the app version and build;
  • iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the screen where the problem occurred;
  • a screenshot, if it does not reveal account or payment information.

Keep secrets private. Never send a password, Apple Account verification code, full payment-card number, or complete purchase receipt.
